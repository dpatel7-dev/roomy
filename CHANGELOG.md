# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- roomy is born. See how full your disk is, tick the easy wins (old installers, app caches, Xcode
  leftovers, package downloads, node_modules in old projects, the Trash) and press Enter.
- Everything goes to the Trash first and **u** (or `roomy putback`) puts it back. Emptying the Trash
  is never ticked for you and always asks first.
- **Biggest folders**: look inside the folders that take the most space, show them in Finder, or move
  one to the Trash.
- `roomy check` lists what could be cleared without changing anything.
